Divergence is signal, not failure

  • When a model misses the administration's totals, there are exactly three causes: the model simplifies, the encoding is wrong, or reality differs from the written rule — advance agreements, rulings, discretion.
  • The ledger separates the first two mechanically: two independent engines, per-case comparison, every residual attributed.
  • What remains — the gap between verified rules and administrative totals — becomes a measurement of the third: how far practice sits from the text. That is a research object no one can currently compute.
  • An overnight feasibility audit even proves which population misses are donor-structure, machine-checked — the specification for the Belgian microdata upgrade.

Simulate — society-wide, in the open

  • policyengine.org/be — reform CIR 92 levers and watch revenue, Gini, poverty, winners and losers, and decile impacts move; a household tab shows net income and marginal rates across the wage distribution.
  • Every figure is computed by the Axiom rules engine from the cited encodings — nothing re-implemented in the page; the parameter set is widening (brackets, family increments, marital quotient, work bonus) with each value engine-computed.
  • Precision is declared on the page, not hidden behind it — the same culture as publishing the degree of precision of a synthetic-data release.

Beyond tax and benefits — encoded this morning

  • Penal: the décimes-additionnels law — every criminal fine ×8 in January 2026, ×10 from 1 February; the amendment caught purely by effective dates, the evidence horizon stated.
  • Contract: the statutory-interest formula of 1865 — computed from ECB Euribor inputs, it reproduces the SPF Finances published 4.50% for 2025 and 2026 to zero basis points. The administration's own publication is the oracle.
  • The computable core of each legal domain is the realistic first scope — and the interpretive boundary is exactly where a law faculty's review bench and test cases turn encodings into scholarship.

Chronicle — the fact layer for Belgium

  • Doctrine: a calibration target is a sum of Chronicle facts — published administrative and national-accounts records, each with source, period, unit, and provenance class. Surveys are validation-only. Aging is a declared ratio of two published facts (FPB outlook pairs), never a guessed uprate.
  • Belgian packages, in the bundle now: Statbel fiscal income distribution 2023 — 14,600 facts (101 income classes × declarations and amounts, national and per region; decile and percentile tax masses with published band bounds; declaration types; professional-income composition) · FPB Economic Outlook 2026–2031 (June 2026) — 1,000 facts across T01/T06/T07/T11/T17/T24 · Statbel population structure 2025 and 2026 · ONSS contributions · ONEM unemployment · SFPD pension caseload · Eurostat tax and social-protection aggregates and household accounts · NBB disposable income · JRC EUROMOD country-report statistics — 90 facts.
  • Scale: the full Chronicle bundle spans 171,836 facts across the US, UK, and Belgium — one ledger, one contract, per-record provenance enforced by CI.
github.com/PolicyEngine/chronicle — the Belgian 2025 vintages merged this weekend.

Microcosm-BE v0.5 — the 254-target surface

  • Fiscal distribution: 202 cells — 101 income classes × {declaration counts, net taxable income}, 2023 administrative levels aged to 2025 by declared FPB fact-ratios.
  • Tax masses: 19 cells — decile 1–9 plus percentile 91–100 total tax, with the published band bounds scaled to 2025 on the same declared ratios; declaration types (joint / individual).
  • Demographics and programs: 18 age × sex × region cells (NUTS1); ONSS worker contributions; SFPD pension recipients; FPB program totals (unemployment, GRAPA, child benefit, pensions); Eurostat self-employment, movable income, household accounts.
  • Discipline: weight-ratio cap 10, fixed seed, effective sample size 1,058; one tax concept per measure (the accrual-basis national-accounts tax total was demoted to validation when it conflicted with the enrolled Statbel decile system — the conflict is documented, not averaged away).
  • The regional surface (956 cells) refused to ship: a machine-checked audit proved joint infeasibility for the US donor — seven region × decile tax cells and the couple ceiling — which is precisely the specification for the Belgian donor pool.

Axiom policy scope for Belgium today

  • Personal income tax (CIR 92): brackets and base tax (art. 130), tax-free amounts and family increments (arts. 131–134), communal and agglomeration surcharges (arts. 465–468), the art. 145-series reductions (pension savings, employer shares, home security, development funds, gifts, domestic employees, childcare, adoption, legal-protection insurance), replacement-income reductions and phase-outs (arts. 147/151/152), credits (arts. 289bis/289ter/289ter/1), company-car benefit in kind.
  • Social security: employee contributions (art. 38 component rates), the work bonus with its 2025 mid-year regimes, the special contribution.
  • Benefits: GRAPA payable amount, social-integration income resource calculation (from the royal decree), child benefit base — with the full regional composition (cohort routing, rank, age and social supplements) encoded and EUROMOD-matched across a 432-case grid, entering on the next corpus release.
  • Counted: 123 rules over 79 provisions live in the app; held encodings (dependants detail, art. 171 separate rates, regional child benefit) are named, tested, and waiting on release promotion — the ladder reports where each sits, and nothing claims completeness.
